THE LAST PAGE

Some stories don't end because the love disappears.
Sometimes they end because one person has to finally let go.

01

What Was My Fault?

You didn't just break me once. You broke me, fixed me, broke me again, and then, somehow, you replaced me with someone who doesn't even know how to care for you the way I did.

And sometimes I still ask myself, what was my fault? Loving you too hard? Caring about you too much? Making sure you were safe, even when I wasn't? Trying every day to make you believe that you were beautiful, that you were worthy, that you were never something to be ashamed of?

I spent so much of myself trying to kill the insecurities you carried. I broke friendships with people close to me because I was scared of ever becoming disloyal to you. I didn't want to become the kind of person who could hurt the girl he loved.

02

Things You Never Asked For

There are so many things you never even asked me to do. I did them because I loved you.

Everyone at home knows how badly I struggle with the heat, how Saim can barely sleep without the AC, but I sacrificed my comfort and my sleep just to stay with you.

I worked until I was exhausted because I dreamed of giving my future wife, Anika, and the little Musa I once imagined, a beautiful life.

I stopped going out with my family when I felt they had disrespected you. I removed girls from my life because I wanted you to feel secure with me. I wanted you to wake up every day knowing, "My husband is not going anywhere."

03

In Your Heart

I even went for Umrah with you in my heart. I made dua for you. I prayed for you when I felt like I had nothing left.

I searched for movies just so we could have another little moment together. I played games with you even when I had work waiting for me, and then sacrificed my sleep to finish that work afterward.

I kept your streaks, found gifts in Adopt Me, and did stupid little things just to make you smile and show you, "Your hubby is enough."

04

I Wanted You To See Yourself Through My Eyes

I spent so much time trying to make you feel beautiful. I knew about the insecurities you carried, even the ones you tried to hide.

I remember knowing that at your childhood sometimes you wanted to hide your face because you didn't feel beautiful enough, and honestly, that broke something inside me.

I wanted to spend my life proving the opposite to you.

I wanted you to look at yourself through my eyes for once.

And even when it came to intimacy, I never saw you as something ordinary. To me, your beauty was something special because you were my Anika.

When you told me about the things you felt insecure about, I wanted to make those insecurities disappear. I wanted you to know that the person I loved could never be someone I would make feel ashamed of herself.

And for all of this, I even started learning things I had never cared about before. I learned about periods because I wanted to know what to do when you were in pain. I wanted to be the person you could depend on even when you couldn't explain what you needed.

And after everything… I still wasn't enough.

That is the part that hurts the most.

Not losing you. Not even being replaced. It's knowing that after everything I gave, you chose to hurt the same person who spent so much time trying to protect your heart.

Maybe the person you chose now will love you. Maybe he will make you happy. I genuinely hope he does. But I know he doesn't know all the little things I knew. He doesn't know every insecurity, every fear, every little thing that could make you smile.

The Things I Gave

Sleep.
Comfort.
Time.
Friendships.
Work.
Money.
Plans.
Duas.
Memories.
Hope.
But I don't regret loving you.
07

One Last Attempt

I even sold my laptop and my gold because I wanted to meet you in Saudi and try one last time to fix everything between us.

I was willing to sacrifice things I owned just for one more chance to make things right.

But maybe some things cannot be fixed by one person.
08

Maybe I Have To Accept That

I spent so much of my life wanting to give my Anika the whole world. But honestly, it was never really about money, gifts, marriage, or anything material.

I just wanted to give you the love I felt you had been missing. I wanted to make you feel safe. I wanted to make you feel beautiful. I wanted you to know that there was at least one person in this world who would look at you and say, "You are enough."

Maybe I was foolish for believing I could love someone enough to heal every wound they carried.

I don't regret the sacrifices.
I don't regret the duas.
I don't regret the sleepless nights.
I don't regret the games, the movies, the gifts.
I don't regret the calls, the websites.
I don't regret the little messages.
I don't regret any of the memories.
I don't regret loving you.

If I ever did something good for you, I hope Allah accepts it. And if I ever hurt you, intentionally or unintentionally, I hope you can forgive me.

Wallahi, my dream was never to hurt you. My dream was to give the whole world to my Anika, my jaanu, my heart, my everything.

But sometimes loving someone also means accepting that you cannot keep holding them when they have already chosen to let go.

So yes…
I'm leaving.
Not because I stopped loving you.
I'm leaving because I loved you.

I'll disappear from your life, and I'll keep making dua and giving charity for your sake for as long as my heart allows me to.

And one day, when I hear that you have found your happiness and built your life with someone else, I'll accept it. Maybe then I'll find someone too, and maybe Allah will write something different for me.

I don't know what the future holds.

I only know that once upon a time,
there was a boy who loved a girl
with everything he had.

And this is the last page of his story with her.

Allah Hafiz, Anika.

I loved you.

And a part of me probably always will.
